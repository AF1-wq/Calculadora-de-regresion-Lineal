## 2024-03-25 - [Supply Chain] **Vulnerability:** polyfill.io CDN was used for polyfills, which has been sold to an untrusted entity and poses a critical supply chain attack risk (serving malicious code). **Learning:** External CDNs for commonly used scripts can change ownership and become attack vectors. Always check the reputation of CDNs and use well-known, trusted providers like Cloudflare, or self-host if possible. **Prevention:** Avoid polyfill.io in favor of trusted alternatives like cdnjs.cloudflare.com/polyfill. Regularly audit external script dependencies.
